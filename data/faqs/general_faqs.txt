FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)

Q1: What are the college timings?
A: College operates from 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM, Monday to Saturday. Saturday has half-day classes until 1:00 PM.

Q2: How do I apply for a bonafide certificate?
A: Visit the administrative office with your ID card. Fill the application form and pay Rs. 50. Certificate will be issued within 3 working days.

Q3: What are the hostel fees?
A: Hostel fees are Rs. 45,000 per year for non-AC rooms and Rs. 75,000 per year for AC rooms. This includes mess charges.

Q4: How do I access the college library?
A: Use your student ID card at the library entrance. Library hours are 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on weekdays and 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM on weekends. Each student can borrow up to 4 books at a time for 14 days.

Q5: What is the fee structure for B.Tech?
A: Tuition fee is Rs. 1,20,000 per year. Additional charges include lab fee (Rs. 10,000), exam fee (Rs. 5,000), and development fee (Rs. 15,000). Total: approximately Rs. 1,50,000 per year.

Q6: Does the college provide placement support?
A: Yes, the Training & Placement cell actively coordinates with 100+ companies. Last year's placement rate was 85% with the highest package of Rs. 24 LPA (CTC).

Q7: How do I apply for a scholarship?
A: Scholarship applications open in August each year. Apply through the college portal under "Scholarships" section. Required documents: income certificate, caste certificate (if applicable), previous semester marksheet.

Q8: What are the sports facilities available?
A: The college has a cricket ground, basketball court, volleyball court, badminton courts, table tennis room, and a gymnasium. Inter-college sports events are held annually.

Q9: Is there a transport facility?
A: Yes, college buses operate on 15 routes covering major areas. Bus fee is Rs. 25,000 per year. Routes and timings are available at the transport office.

Q10: How do I get my semester exam hall ticket?
A: Hall tickets are available on the college portal 1 week before exams. Ensure all dues are cleared and attendance requirements are met before downloading.
