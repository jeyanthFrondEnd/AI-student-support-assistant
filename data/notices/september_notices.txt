COLLEGE NOTICE BOARD — SEPTEMBER 2024

NOTICE #1: MID-SEMESTER EXAMINATIONS
Date: September 5, 2024
The mid-semester examinations for all departments will be conducted from September 20, 2024 to September 28, 2024. The detailed timetable is available on the college website. Students are advised to collect their hall tickets from the examination cell.

NOTICE #2: HACKATHON — CODE SPRINT 2024
Date: September 3, 2024
The Department of CSE is organizing "Code Sprint 2024" — a 24-hour hackathon on October 5-6, 2024. Registration is open until September 25. Team size: 2-4 members. Registration fee: Rs. 200 per team. Register at the CSE department office or online portal.

NOTICE #3: HOLIDAY ANNOUNCEMENT
Date: September 1, 2024
The college will remain closed on September 15, 2024 (Sunday) and September 16, 2024 (Monday) on account of Ganesh Chaturthi. Regular classes resume on September 17, 2024.

NOTICE #4: LIBRARY FINE AMNESTY WEEK
Date: August 28, 2024
The library is waiving all overdue fines from September 10-17, 2024. Students with pending books are encouraged to return them during this period. After September 17, regular fines of Rs. 5 per day per book will apply.

NOTICE #5: PLACEMENT DRIVE — TCS
Date: September 4, 2024
TCS will be conducting a campus placement drive on September 22, 2024. Eligible branches: CSE, ECE, EEE, IT. Minimum CGPA: 6.0. No active backlogs allowed. Register on the placement portal by September 15. Pre-placement talk on September 21 at 3:00 PM in the main auditorium.
