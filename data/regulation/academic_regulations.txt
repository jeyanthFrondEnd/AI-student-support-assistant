COLLEGE ACADEMIC REGULATIONS (2024-2025)

1. ATTENDANCE POLICY
   - Minimum 75% attendance is mandatory for all subjects.
   - Students with less than 65% attendance will be detained and not allowed to write exams.
   - Students between 65-75% attendance may apply for condonation with a fine of Rs. 500.
   - Medical leave requires a valid medical certificate submitted within 7 days.

2. EXAMINATION RULES
   - Internal exams carry 30 marks and external/semester exams carry 70 marks.
   - Students must score a minimum of 40% in each subject to pass.
   - Maximum 3 re-valuation requests per semester, with Rs. 200 per subject fee.
   - Malpractice during exams leads to debarment from exams for 1-2 semesters.
   - Use of mobile phones during exams is strictly prohibited.

3. GRADING SYSTEM
   - O Grade: 90-100 (Grade Point: 10)
   - A+ Grade: 80-89 (Grade Point: 9)
   - A Grade: 70-79 (Grade Point: 8)
   - B+ Grade: 60-69 (Grade Point: 7)
   - B Grade: 50-59 (Grade Point: 6)
   - C Grade: 40-49 (Grade Point: 5)
   - F Grade: Below 40 (Fail)

4. ACADEMIC INTEGRITY
   - Plagiarism in assignments will result in zero marks.
   - Second offense leads to disciplinary action.
   - All assignments must be submitted through the anti-plagiarism portal.

5. CREDIT REQUIREMENTS
   - B.Tech students must complete a minimum of 160 credits for graduation.
   - Each semester typically has 20-24 credits.
   - Students must complete at least 2 open electives from other departments.
   - Mandatory internship of 4 weeks after 6th semester (2 credits).
